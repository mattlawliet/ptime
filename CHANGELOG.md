# Changelog

## 2.0.2

- **Labelled times:** your own time shows once under the search bar; each card shows the player's time with "16h ahead", "6h behind" or "same time as you" instead of a second, unlabelled clock.
- **Less repetition:** each card names the zone and its UTC offset once, and clocks drop the seconds.
- **Heart favorites:** the Unicode star is replaced by the vanilla heart sprite, with a larger click area and a tooltip.
- **Layout:** cards line up with the header, a vanilla scrollbar shows when the list overflows, and Up/Down/PageUp/PageDown scroll it. Compact mode is a true two-line card.
- **Search hint:** the search box shows "Search players" while empty.
- **Sharper skins:** the hat layer draws at the face's size, so both stay at a whole-number scale.
- **Offset check:** `/ptime` rejects bare UTC offsets outside -12 to +14.
